FAQ

Not answered here? Ask in the Builders Telegram Group.

I don't know blockchain yet. Can I join?

Yes! You do not need to know blockchain beforehand. UniHack is for beginners too. You get free learning material and mentors to help while you build.

Who can join UniHack?

UniHack is for university students in Vietnam, from any major.
You can join alone or form a team of up to 3 people.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is completely free.
You can build and test your project on the Avalanche Fuji Testnet without using real money.

I can't code. Can I still join?

Absolutely.
UniHack is not only for developers. You can contribute in UI/UX, Product, Business, AI, Data, Marketing, Pitching or other skills.

Do I have to have a team?

No. You can join alone, or find teammates after you register.
You can find teammates in the Participants list, at events or in the Builders Telegram Group.

Do I have to attend in person?

No. You can take part in UniHack fully online.
You can register, find a team, build your product and submit everything online.
Only if you make the Top 10 do you need to attend the Final Demo Day in person, to pitch and demo your product to the judges.

What is the Builder Pass?

A side minigame, separate from the hackathon prizes.
Finish 5 quests on the Builder Pass page to enter the draw. 50 builders are drawn and share $500 ($10 each).
Winners are announced at the Final Demo Day.

Do I have to pick one of the listed topics?

No. The topics are only suggestions.
You can pick one, combine several, or build your own idea.
Your project just needs to follow the rules and run on the Avalanche Fuji Testnet.

Can I bring a project I am already building?

Yes. A class project, a side project or a startup idea all count, including a web2 app.
Put one real part of it on Avalanche: a smart contract, payments, an NFT, a token or wallet sign-in.
That part is what the 25 points for Avalanche Integration score.

What should I build?

Anything that solves a real problem and is built on Avalanche.

Where do I submit?

Here on team1vn.xyz. Sign in, open your profile page and fill in Submit Project.
Any member of the team can do it. You can save a draft and come back; it counts once you press Submit.
You submit as a team, and a team can be one person.

Submissions close at 05:59 on 25 November 2026 (Vietnam time).

How does the final work?

After the online round, 10 teams are chosen for the Final Demo Day.
Teams pitch live, demo their product and answer questions from the judges.

How much is the prize?

The 10 finalist teams share $3,000 at the Final Demo Day: 30,000,000 VND for first, 20,000,000 VND for second, 11,000,000 VND for third, and 2,000,000 VND for each of the other seven teams.
Another $500 goes to 50 builders through the Builder Pass draw ($10 each).
